About us

Expertise for your peace of mind

Benjamin Auto Mechanic is a family-owned auto repair shop where honesty, integrity, and good business are as important to us as to our customers. We hold our work to the highest standard in the automotive industry.

We have been serving the Hillsboro area for more than 20 years. Our affordable service and customer satisfaction have expanded our customer base to Beaverton, Portland, Tigard, Sherwood, Forest Grove and Cornelius.

Expertise for your peace of mind: you can rest assured that you and your car will be well cared for, at our shop and once you're back on the road.
