Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

This page explains what information jaronwenger.com collects and what I do with it.

When you contact me

If you call, text or email me, I get the information you send, like your name, phone number, email address and anything you write about your business. I use it only to reply to you and to work on your website. I don't sell or share it with anyone else.

Contact form

If you fill out the form on my homepage, I get your name, business name, phone number, email address and message. It's saved in HubSpot, the customer relationship management (CRM) tool I use to keep track of leads, and a copy is emailed to me through Web3Forms. I use it only to contact you about your website. See HubSpot's privacy policy and Web3Forms' privacy policy.

Analytics and cookies

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it, like which pages are viewed, how people found the site, and whether they tap to call, text or email. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device, browser and the pages you visit. It doesn't tell me who you are, and I don't use it for advertising.

My homepage also uses HubSpot's tracking code, which sets cookies to record the pages you view and how you found the site. If you fill out the contact form, that visit history is linked to the information you submit.

Google processes this data under Google's privacy policy. You can see how Google uses data from sites that use its services, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

Hosting

This site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Your information

You can ask me what information I have about you, or ask me to delete it, at any time.
