About us

A family that takes pride in its work

Pacific Sky Remodeling is a family-owned company run by Marco and Scarlette, with Marco Sr. and Marco Jr. on the job.

Our legacy goes back 30 years, from painting emergency ladder access in New York City to office buildings on the busy streets of Los Angeles. We finally found home in Portland, Oregon, where we live and work today.

We paint homes and businesses inside and out, and we take care of the small details that make a paint job last.
