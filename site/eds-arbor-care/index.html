About us

Two friends, one goal

Ed's Arbor Care was started in 2010 by two friends, Edward Dreske II and Jason Banzer, with one idea: incredible service at an incredible price, treating every customer like family.

Ed grew up working on his father's tree crews, served in the Marines, and became an ISA Certified Arborist. Jason worked his way up from raking leaves and dragging brush to pruning from bucket trucks and supervising crews.

We're owner operated, members of the Tree Care Industry Association, and licensed, bonded and insured in Oregon.
