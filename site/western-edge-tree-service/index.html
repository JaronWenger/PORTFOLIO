About Scott

Trees, rigging and machinery, all in one place

Trees, high-angle rigging and machinery have been recurring themes for Scott Paider, and Western Edge Tree Service is where they all come together.

From USFS wildland fire crew to welding and steel construction, Scott has put his hands to work in the air and on the ground for many years. After a decade rigging highline camera systems and stunt safety in the film industry, he brought together his working knowledge of forestry, rigging and high-angle safety in the form of Western Edge.

Scott has been a Gorge resident and arborist since 1995 and started Western Edge in 2007. We have certified arborists on staff, are members of the International Society of Arboriculture, and are licensed, bonded and insured in Oregon and Washington.
