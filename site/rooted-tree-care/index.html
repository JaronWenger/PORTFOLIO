Meet the owner

Eric Smith, ISA Certified Arborist

In 2003 I packed up all of my belongings and moved from my home in a small coastal New England town in search of trees, and boy did I find them. I spent that spring working as a custodian to the great coastal Redwoods in Northern California. At that point I knew trees were my future.

I then moved up to Portland, started a life with friends, met my wife and started a family. Portland is where I transformed my love for trees into a career, and soon after I earned my ISA Arborist Certification.

Rooted Tree Care is a continuation of my passion and stewardship towards trees. With nearly 20 years of experience working with trees, we're happy to help you with your arboricultural needs.
