About us

Adam and his crew

Adams Auto is Adam's shop on West 2nd Street in The Dalles. Locals bring their cars here for honest repairs, and plenty of travelers towed off I-84 have left as fans.

From engine diagnostics and alternators to A/C and Subarus, Adam and his crew figure out what's wrong and get you back on the road.
