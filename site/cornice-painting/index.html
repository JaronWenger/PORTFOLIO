Meet the owner

Ray Culbertson

Cornice Painting LLC offers high-quality residential specialty painting based in Hood River, Oregon. Ray has nearly a decade of painting experience and provides quick and accurate personalized quotes.

Ray began painting at age 19, but pursued engineering and business degrees before finding his passion for high-end interior and exterior painting work. When you hire Cornice Painting you are teaming up with a well-educated and experienced professional that will personally work on your property.

Our lead paint certification allows us to work on homes built prior to 1978 as well as newer-built construction. Ray is also an artist available for small or large-scale artwork such as murals and large canvas paintings.
