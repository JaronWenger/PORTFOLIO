About us

Local to The Dalles

Pray Electric & Construction's foundation is built on quality electricians with many years of experience, strong work ethic and integrity. We value the importance of building relationships with our clients. Electric work is not just what we do, it's who we are, and we're proud of it.

Owner and electrical contractor Larry Pray is a local from The Dalles with over 35 years of electrical experience. Over the years he worked his way up through local electrical contractors as foreman, project manager, estimator and electrical designer, and he still works in the field supporting his fellow journeymen. In 2021, Pray Electric & Construction LLC was born.

And then there's Kimber, a silver lab who thinks she is an electrician. You'll often see her with her head out the window of Larry's work truck.
