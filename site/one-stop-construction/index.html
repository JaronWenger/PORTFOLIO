About One Stop

Quality work at a reasonable rate

One Stop Construction & Handyman Services was founded in 1997 with one fundamental goal: do quality work at a reasonable rate. The rest is history!

Now in business for 20+ years and counting, we have built our business on repeat customers and positive word of mouth.

We are locally owned and operated and complete most projects using our own crews, so quality control is held to the highest standard. We pride ourselves on the work we complete.
