About us

Built on integrity, driven by heart

Columbia GCS was founded by Jameson Dexter and Scot Braley, two friends and business partners with a shared belief in fairness, honesty and trust.

From day one, the goal has been simple: treat every customer like family. That means showing up on time, doing the job right, and always being transparent with our work and pricing.

Whether it's a quick repair or a full system installation, you can count on expert craftsmanship, fair pricing and a team that truly cares.
