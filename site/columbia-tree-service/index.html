About us

Local tree care since 1992

Columbia Tree Service is a locally owned, professional tree care contractor serving residential and commercial clients in Hood River, The Dalles and the surrounding communities. We've been in business since 1992.

Whether it's tree or hedge trimming, we have the tools and the knowledge to do the job right the first time. We follow the ISA guidelines for tree trimming, so you never have to worry about your trees being incorrectly pruned, and we have a fleet of aerial trucks and climbers for hazard trees over your house.

Owner Kris Zorza is hands-on with every job. Our objective is to provide our customers with the highest quality and fastest service.
