FAQ

Questions

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the form below.

What does it cost?

$750 for the website (launch price), then $21/mo or $210/yr for hosting. Edits are included with hosting.

What if I don't like the site you build?

Then you don't pay anything. You see the finished site before we ever talk money. And if you say yes but don't love it within 30 days, I refund you and help you switch back to what you had.

What do I need to do?

One call. We connect your domain, Google Business Profile, CRM and Google Analytics access together. I handle everything else.

Will it help me show up on Google?

Every service gets its own page, and I set up Search Console and link the site to your Google Business Profile, so Google knows what you do and where.

Do I own the domain?

Yes. You own your domain.

Can I edit it myself?

Yes, with a simple email login. Edits are also included with hosting, so you can just text me what to change.

What if I already have a website?

That's fine. I build your new site first, so you can compare. If you say yes, we connect your domain on one 20-minute call. If you don't love it within 30 days, I refund you and help you switch back to what you had.

What happens if I leave?

You keep your domain, and you get your files.
