Our history

Moving the Gorge since 1930

Lester Auto Freight / Moving & Storage was first established in 1930 by Dick Lester. Over the years it was an agent for companies like Atlas and Mayflower.

In 1985, Lester Moving & Storage was taken over by our current President, Clinton Adams.

Residential or commercial, across town or across the country, there's no job too big or too small.
