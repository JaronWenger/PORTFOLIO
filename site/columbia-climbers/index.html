About us

John and Karen's family business

Columbia Climbers is a local, family-run tree service in Washougal, owned by John and Karen Detally.

For over two decades we've taken care of trees across the Columbia River Gorge, from single trims to big fir removals on forested properties. Some of our customers have been calling us for more than 15 years.

We work with homeowners and businesses, and every job starts with a free consultation so we can make a plan that fits your property.
