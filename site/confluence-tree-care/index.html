Meet Mark

Mark Trevithick, owner & ISA Certified Arborist

Mark has been climbing in some form for 20 years, starting as a rock climber and climbing guide in Moab, Utah, then as an industrial rope access technician working up to Level 3 Supervisor.

He and Briana settled in Washougal in 2014, bought six acres, and started planting trees. Climbing and pruning their own trees, planting an orchard and reclaiming blackberry thickets with native species led straight to Confluence Tree Care.

Today Mark is an ISA Board Certified Arborist, licensed in Washington and Oregon, bonded and insured, and certified Level 3 with both SPRAT and IRATA.
