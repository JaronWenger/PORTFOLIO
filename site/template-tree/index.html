About us

Locally owned in Hood River

Highline Tree Co. is a small, local crew taking care of trees for homes and properties across the Columbia Gorge.

Big removals near houses, careful pruning and stump grinding: we plan every job before the first cut, protect your yard while we work, and haul everything away when we're done.
