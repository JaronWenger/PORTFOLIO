Who we are

Locally owned, right here in the Gorge

Columbia River Gorge Concrete and Excavation LLC is a locally owned and operated concrete and excavation contractor proudly serving the Columbia River Gorge and surrounding communities in Washington and Oregon.

With years of hands-on industry experience, our team is committed to high-quality workmanship, dependable service and efficient project completion.

We're fully licensed, bonded and insured in both Washington (#605515910) and Oregon (#251600), and we follow strict safety standards on every job.
