About Continuum

Eric Bock, owner and creative artist

Continuum Landscape was born from a passion for sustainability, creativity and beauty. Owner and creative artist Eric Bock has a love for the outdoors, our planet, and creating with his hands.

He has extensive knowledge in plants, rock work, irrigation, water feature installation and repair, lighting, drainage, hardscaping, carpentry, sustainability and permaculture, food forests and habitat restoration. His designs and installations are rooted in over 23 years of landscape experience and world travel.

From simple to intricate, we help you envision and create your perfect outdoor space.
