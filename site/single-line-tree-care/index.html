About us

Local, careful and quick to quote

Single Line Tree Care is a Hillsboro tree service run by Chet, with Angel leading the crew in the field.

We specialize in high-risk trimming and removal: tall firs next to houses, trees over fences and decks, and trees along busy roads. We take extra care to protect your landscaping while we work.

Chet comes out to look at your trees, gives you a fair quote on the spot, and gets you on the schedule.
