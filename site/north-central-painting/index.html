Meet the owner

Ben Johnson, owner + professional house painter

North Central Painting is Ben Johnson's company, licensed in Oregon since 2019 and based in Gresham.

Ben does the work himself, with the residential painting knowledge and experience to tackle your project and help transform your home. Every job comes with the same promise of quality, attention to detail, dedication and durability.

We only use top-quality materials: Sherwin-Williams, Miller Paint, Benjamin Moore, 3M, FrogTape, Purdy, Wooster and more. Call or text Ben any time.
