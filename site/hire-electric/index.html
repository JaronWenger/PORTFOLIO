About us

Electrifying the Gorge since 1935

Hire Electric Inc. are electrical contractors that are fully licensed, bonded and insured to perform work in both Oregon and Washington. Hire Electric was established in the late 1930s and was incorporated in 1974.

We normally have 15 to 25 electricians at any one time, plus an office support staff. Our electricians complete a 5-year training program and keep up a continuing education program every year. Most of our journeyman electricians completed their apprenticeships through the NECA-IBEW Training Center in Portland.

Hire Electric performs electrical contracting work within approximately a 100-mile radius of The Dalles, Oregon.
