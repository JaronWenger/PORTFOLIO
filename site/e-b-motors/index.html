About us

Looking for the right repair?

Have you been looking for affordable repair and for certified mechanics that will perform your repair just right? E & B Motors specializes in many different repairs, and we will gladly help you get what you need.

From timing belts, maintenance and A/C to engine repair, tires, breakdowns and battery changes, we service all makes and models, plus motorcycles.
