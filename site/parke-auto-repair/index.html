About Jamie

Meet Jamie Parke

Jamie Parke learned the trade from his dad, Jerry, and worked at The Dalles Auto Sales and Point S Tires before opening his own shop on East 2nd Street, across from Big Jim's.

His approach to diagnosis is simple: scanners and listening. Most cars are diagnosed within a day or two.
