Our team

High quality tree care

Green Leaf Tree Service pays special attention to the details of our work to ensure the complete satisfaction of each client. We will make sure your trees are properly taken care of.

Every job starts with an ISA certified arborist on site, evaluating tree health, structural integrity and the surrounding property, followed by a detailed written proposal. Once you accept, we plan the work for safety and efficiency, perform it with low-impact techniques and controlled section lowering, then haul away and chip the debris. Billing is sent after the work is completed.

Founder Rafael Lucio Jimenez leads the crew, with ISA certified arborists Rick McKinney (residential, ISA #PN-7583A) and Adam Haas (commercial, ISA #WI-0943A).

Need an arborist consultation?
