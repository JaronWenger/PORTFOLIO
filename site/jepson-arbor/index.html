About

Meet Cody Jepson

Jepson Arbor Specialists is Cody Jepson's company. Cody is a certified arborist with 28 years in tree care.

Our goal is simple: quality, high-end arborist services without the high-end price, for homeowners and HOAs across the greater Portland area.

Many of our customers have us back year after year to check on the health of their trees, and we'll always give you the facts without pressure.
