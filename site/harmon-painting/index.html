About

Meet Corey Harmon

Harmon Painting is Corey Harmon's company, based in The Dalles and serving Wasco County.

Corey paints homes inside and out for residential and small commercial customers, and he's certified for lead-based paint renovation, so older homes are in good hands.

Harmon Painting is licensed with the Oregon CCB, bonded and insured.
