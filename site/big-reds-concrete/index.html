Meet the owner

John "Big Red" Kalamafoni

John started out working for his father's concrete contracting company at the age of 12, and worked with him into his early 20s.

He then spent over a decade in commercial concrete before starting Big Red's Concrete in 2020.

His nickname reflects not only his towering presence but his dedication to delivering big results on every project, from a new driveway to a decorative patio.
