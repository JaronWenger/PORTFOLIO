About us

A small company ready for big jobs

Dig It Construction was established in 2017 in Sandy, Oregon, with a focus on dependable excavation and construction services for homeowners, property owners and commercial customers.

Construction has always been a part of the family. Before starting Dig It, Tanner spent years working in excavation and construction, and that hands-on experience became the foundation for a company focused on doing the job right, communicating with customers, and taking on projects of all sizes.
