Our story

Local, family owned since 2004

Beam Excavating is a local, family-owned business operating in the entire Columbia Gorge. We started in 2004 with smaller projects and grew into significant undertakings, including our biggest project to date: a waterline stretching through downtown Hood River.

Chad Beam, owner and founder, is from Hood River and keeps great relationships with his clients. Our crew brings decades of experience in septic systems, precision grading, pipe work and commercial projects.

Because we are local, we support local. Beam backs fundraisers, high school events and local causes, because we believe in growing our community one job at a time.
