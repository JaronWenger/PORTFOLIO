Our story

A team that treats you like family

Brent C. Hofer Painting started as a local, family-owned Oregon City business. Brent now employs the Texta family to run daily operations, and our team has over 50 years of painting experience between them. Many of us are family.

Every job starts with Brent: he meets you, looks at the work and emails a free written estimate. For exteriors, we pressure wash and caulk first and paint five or more days later.

We use Miller Paint, made right here in the Northwest, and we donate painting jobs to auctions that support schools, children's hospitals and programs for kids.
