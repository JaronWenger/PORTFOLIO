Meet the owner

Josh Woolsey, ISA Certified Arborist

Josh started out in the tree industry over twenty years ago with Asplund Tree Experts as a line clearance arborist. After years of working for different companies in Oregon, he branched out on his own and started Gorge Arbor Care in 2019.

We're a locally owned family business, and Josh has strived to create a business that is local and affordable for everyone. He's an ISA Certified Arborist, and Gorge Arbor Care is licensed and bonded with the Construction Contractors Board and insured by State Farm.

Josh works alongside a hard-working crew: Justin, Troy and Devon. We work safely and effectively to meet your specific needs, and our estimates are always free.
